<objective>
Unify the six scattered table settings under one `table` section with a global default and a
per-app override, resolved by a single pure function every board-rendering page calls (REQ-04,
D-13).

Purpose: three of these settings are top-level globals today, three are buried in a trainer's own
section, and each new table app would otherwise add a fourth and fifth copy of the same six
questions. One schema plus one resolver means the statistical lab (plan 01-07) inherits sane
defaults without adding a settings surface of its own.

Output: `features/settings/tableSettings.ts`, a `table` section in the store behind a bumped
persist version, and every board page reading through the resolver.
</objective>
