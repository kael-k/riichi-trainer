<objective>
Ship the statistical lab: a standalone table app that loads or authors a wall, plays only your own
discards, and shows the complete ukeire ranking and the complete danger-tier breakdown for the
hand in front of you, with nothing graded (REQ-05, D-15, D-16).

Purpose: every number the lab shows is already computed inside the existing trainers and thrown
away after one or two rows are rendered. The lab is a rendering surface over engine output, not new
engine work — and it has to be its own route because a panel inside folding would collide with that
drill's rules about danger markers and threat hands.

Output: `features/lab/`, a `/lab` route, a home-page card in the Table group, and locale copy in all
four languages.
</objective>
