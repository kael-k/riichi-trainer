<objective>
Split the efficiency trainer's single opponents-checkbox route into two honest routed apps —
solitaire and table — on top of `useTableRound`, and delete the `opponents` flag from the settings
store, the URL codec and the round options (REQ-01, D-01, D-02, D-03).

Purpose: a checkbox that silently turns one drill into a different drill is the thing this phase
exists to remove. Two routes make the choice legible on the home page, let each app keep the
layout that actually suits it, and give the lab a third consumer of the same hook to copy.

Output: `features/efficiency/` becomes the table app, `features/efficiency-solo/` is the new
solitaire app, both grading through one shared pure module.
</objective>
