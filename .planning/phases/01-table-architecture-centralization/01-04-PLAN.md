<objective>
Rebuild `useFoldingRound` on `core/table.ts`'s pure primitives — its own thin hook, not
`useTableRound` — and close the live reveal bug that lets a global setting show a threat's hand
mid-drill (REQ-07, REQ-06, D-08, D-14).

Purpose: folding's control flow genuinely differs (its riichi-target policy flip runs *between*
`beginTurn` and `finishTurn`, which `useTableRound`'s three callbacks cannot express), so it
composes the shared primitives directly instead of being forced through a contract built for
someone else. Everything that is *not* different — visibility, the go-round, the snapshot, the
replay — stops being a second implementation.

Output: a folding hook with no duplicated engine logic, a wall-backed link, and a reveal gate that
lives below the settings layer.
</objective>
